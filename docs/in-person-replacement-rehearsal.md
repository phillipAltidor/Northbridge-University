# In-person replacement rehearsal — November 8, 2026

Staging only. Active database, schema, completed grades and identities unchanged.

Reviewed all 138 affected Fall 2026 local sections: 115 non-in-person sections
and 23 documented conversion sections. Seventy have reviewed in-person source
alternatives; 68 need course resequencing or source/registration review. The
machine-readable plan contains each affected course/group and its official
section alternatives, including source CRNs, labels, credits and all meetings.
Historical/alias meeting templates retain null official CRNs.

A straight replacement is not feasible. A conservative partial rehearsal
removed 870 Fall attempts across 31 courses with no verified usable offering,
leaving 296 students below their minimum loads. Five of those course keys have
possible Spring in-person options; these still need full Spring validation.
This is a diagnostic experiment, not a recommended removal of registrations.

The timetable solver proved the remaining fixed course combinations infeasible.
Four pairwise course conflicts are recorded in the plan. Faculty/room assignments
and final seat calculations are therefore not certified. Fifty fresh source
sections with linked/cross-list metadata were conservatively excluded pending
review because existing registration logic does not enforce their coupling.
No schema alteration is proposed or applied.

The independent prerequisite review preserves all 34,682 completed-history
attempts, but finds 32 Spring failures after those experimental deferrals.
Pending work remains conditional. No earned grades or completion credit were
invented. The diagnostic candidate is explicitly blocked from activation.
